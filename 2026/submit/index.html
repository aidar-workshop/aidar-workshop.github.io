About AIDaRS

AIDaRS is an optional reviewer-anonymous pilot that complements the required OpenReview submission with a checked, machine-readable copy for reproducibility and more detailed feedback. Authors can expect local privacy checks, no retained project files on the server, private versioned review on GitHub, and the ability to read and answer reviews with the aidar tool.
